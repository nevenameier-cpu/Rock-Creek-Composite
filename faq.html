Frequently asked questions

Student-athletes in participating grade levels (typically 6th–12th grade, per your NICA league rules) can join. No prior racing experience is required. As a composite team, riders from multiple schools are welcome.

All riders register through NICA at nationalmtb.org. Select Rock Creek Composite as your team. See our registration page for step-by-step guidance.

A functioning mountain bike with knobby tires—hardtail or full suspension. Bikes should have working brakes and no loose parts. Coaches can help assess fit at the first practice. Avoid road bikes or BMX for trail practices.

Racing is encouraged but not required to be part of the team. Many riders start with practices only and add races when they feel ready. NICA races are inclusive—there is no bench.

Costs include NICA registration, league fees, and team dues. Some leagues and teams offer financial assistance—ask coaches if cost is a barrier.

See our practice information page for current days, times, and trail locations. Always check the events calendar for weather cancellations.

We need volunteers for practices, race weekends, fundraising, and trail stewardship. Parents can also become NICA-certified coaches. Contact us through the contact page to volunteer.

Coaches teach skills progressively, require helmets, and group riders by ability. Like any sport, there is risk, but NICA emphasizes risk management and proper technique. Riders start on appropriate terrain and build up.
