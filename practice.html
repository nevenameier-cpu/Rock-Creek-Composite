Locations

Where we ride

Primary trail network

Rock Creek area trail system — parking and meet-up details will be posted before each season.

Alternate location

Used during wet conditions or when primary trails are closed. Coaches notify families by email and calendar.

What to bring

  • Helmet, bike, water, and snack
  • Parent/guardian check-in for new riders
  • Weather layers—practices run unless unsafe conditions
  • Positive attitude and readiness to learn trail etiquette

Weather policy

Practices may be shortened, relocated, or canceled for lightning, extreme heat, ice, or trail closures. Coaches send updates via the team calendar and email. When in doubt, check events before heading out.
