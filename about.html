About Rock Creek Composite

A youth mountain bike team built around riding, learning, and belonging.

Who we are

Rock Creek Composite brings together middle and high school student-athletes from across our community who share a love of mountain biking and the outdoors. Whether a rider is brand new to the sport or already chasing their next challenge, our goal is the same: create a place where every rider can grow, have fun, and find their people.

Our mission

Strong riders. Stronger character.

Rock Creek Composite exists to help young people discover what they are capable of, on the bike and beyond it.

Through mountain biking, riders build skills, confidence, resilience, independence, and friendships. We compete as part of the Oregon Interscholastic Cycling League and share NICA's commitment to fun, inclusivity, equity, respect, and community.

As a composite team, we bring together riders from different schools and communities. There are no tryouts and no need for prior racing experience. Coaches meet riders where they are, group them by ability and experience, and help each athlete progress at their own pace.

What we believe

Five values. One team.

Fun

Mountain biking should be something kids look forward to. We create opportunities for adventure, friendship, challenge, and plenty of time to simply enjoy being on two wheels.

Inclusivity

Everyone belongs here. Riders come with different backgrounds, abilities, experience levels, and goals, and every athlete deserves to feel welcomed, supported, and respected.

Equity

We work to make mountain biking accessible and give every student-athlete meaningful opportunities to participate, learn, and grow.

Respect

We respect our teammates, coaches, volunteers, trails, other trail users, and ourselves. Safe riding and responsible trail stewardship are part of being a mountain biker.

Community

A great team is bigger than the riders. Parents, coaches, volunteers, and supporters all help create the culture that makes youth mountain biking special.

A typical season

Ride. Learn. Grow. Repeat.

Our season brings together practices, skills development, trail riding, team-building, and Oregon League event weekends.

Practices give riders time to build bike-handling skills, fitness, confidence, and trail awareness while getting to know their teammates. When event weekends arrive, riders can put those skills to the test in a supportive environment surrounded by teammates, families, coaches, and the broader Oregon mountain biking community.

Riders choose their own level of challenge. There are no cuts and no bench. Whether a student-athlete is working toward their first event or looking to push themselves further, there is a place for them on our team.
