Training for coaches

Want to coach with us?

We're currently looking for more Level 2 coaches to join Rock Creek Composite.

NICA provides a structured coach education program designed to give coaches the skills and confidence to teach young riders effectively and create safe, inclusive experiences on the bike.

OTB 101 is required for all Level 2 and Level 3 coaches. The training combines online education with an in-person skills clinic and gives coaches practical tools for teaching fundamental mountain bike skills.
