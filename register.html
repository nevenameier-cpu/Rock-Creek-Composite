Join the Team

New riders welcome

Join Rock Creek Composite

No racing experience required. Just bring a bike, a helmet, and a willingness to ride. Rock Creek Composite welcomes middle and high school student-athletes of all experience and ability levels. Whether your rider is brand new to mountain biking or already loves the trails, our coaches will help them build skills, confidence, and a love of riding.

Official registration

All student-athletes must complete registration through NICA before participating in team practices or Oregon League events.

Register through NICA

Getting started is easy

1. Create your NICA account

Student-athletes and parents complete registration through NICA's registration system. Registration includes the required waivers, insurance, and league participation requirements.

2. Select Rock Creek Composite

When prompted during registration, choose Rock Creek Composite as your team.

3. Connect with our coaches

Once registration is complete, reach out to the coaching team and let us know about your rider's experience level. We'll help you understand what to expect at the first practice and make sure your rider is placed in the right group.
